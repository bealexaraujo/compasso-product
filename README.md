# Compasso
### AI-powered tax compliance for independent psychologists

Compasso is a product concept focused on reducing the complexity of tax compliance for independent psychologists in Brazil.

The product is designed to help professionals understand what they need to pay, when they need to pay, and confirm that it was completed, without requiring constant manual oversight.

**Status:** MVP in development

---

## The Problem

Independent professionals are responsible for managing their own tax obligations, but the process is complex, fragmented across multiple government systems, and rarely taught.

Users may need to:

- Understand which obligations apply to them
- Remember payment deadlines
- Calculate or verify amounts
- Keep track of completed payments
- Store and validate payment receipts
- Deal with uncertainty around whether an obligation was correctly completed

The result is unnecessary cognitive load and a high risk of missed deadlines or incomplete compliance.

---

## The Product

Compasso explores an AI-first approach to tax compliance.

Instead of requiring users to continuously navigate a dashboard, the product is designed around an agent that initiates contact before deadlines (via WhatsApp) and validates completion when the user sends back a proof document.

A simplified interaction might look like:

> **Compasso:** Your INSS payment is due tomorrow.
>
> **User:** *(Uploads payment receipt.)*
>
> **Compasso:** Payment identified and validated. Your obligation is marked as complete.

The goal is to make compliance feel less like administrative work and more like an automated workflow the professional can trust.

---

## Target Users

The initial product focus is independent professionals who manage their own tax obligations and do not have a hired accountant.

The first use case was developed around **independent psychologists in Brazil**, with the product architecture designed to potentially support other regulated professions in future phases.
