# Compasso

### AI-powered tax compliance for independent professionals

Compasso is a product concept focused on reducing the complexity of tax compliance for independent professionals through an AI-powered assistant.

The product is designed to help professionals understand what they need to pay, when they need to pay it, and whether their obligations have been completed — without requiring them to navigate complex tax processes themselves.

> **Status:** MVP in development

---

## The Problem

Independent professionals are responsible for managing their own tax obligations, but the process is often fragmented, repetitive, and difficult to understand.

Users may need to:

* Understand which obligations apply to them
* Remember payment deadlines
* Calculate or verify amounts
* Keep track of completed payments
* Store and validate payment receipts
* Deal with uncertainty around whether an obligation was correctly completed

The result is unnecessary cognitive load and a high risk of missed deadlines or incomplete compliance.

---

## The Product

Compasso explores an AI-first approach to tax compliance.

Instead of requiring users to continuously navigate a dashboard, the product is designed around an assistant that proactively helps users manage their obligations.

A simplified interaction might look like:

**Compasso:**
Your INSS payment is due tomorrow.

**User:**
*Uploads payment receipt.*

**Compasso:**
Payment identified and validated. Your obligation is marked as complete.

The goal is to make compliance feel less like administrative work and more like an automated workflow.

---

## Target Users

The initial product focus is independent professionals who manage their own tax obligations and do not have dedicated financial or accounting support.

The first use case was developed around **independent psychologists in Brazil**, with the product architecture designed to potentially support other professional segments over time.
