# Problem

## The Situation

In Brazil, autonomous professionals — freelancers and independent contractors
operating as **Pessoa Física (PF)** — are fully responsible for managing their
own tax compliance. There is no employer to withhold taxes, no HR department, 
no payroll system. Just the individual, a set of obligations, and a fragmented
landscape of government portals.

For autonomous psychologists, this means handling at minimum:

| Obligation | Authority | Frequency |
|---|---|---
| Carnê-Leão | Receita Federal | Monthly |
| INSS Plano Simplificado | Previdência Social | Monthly |
| Receita Saúde | Receita Federal | Weekly / Monthly |
| DIRPF | Receita Federal | Annual (Apr 30) |
| Anuidade CRP | Federal Council of Psychology | Annual |
| CCM | Municipal government (São Paulo) | Registration + renewal |

Each obligation lives on a different government portal, has its own login,
its own deadline logic, and its own proof of completion. A professional who
misses a deadline doesn't just pay late, they pay fines, lose compliance
status, and risk professional standing with the CRP.

## The Problem

Independent psychologists are managing complex, multi-source fiscal compliance
with tools that were never built for them.

The process today looks like this:

- **Discover** which obligations apply to their specific profile (PF vs PJ,
  with or without CCM, with or without Receita Saúde volume, etc.)
- **Track deadlines** across six different systems, none of which talk to
  each other
- **Calculate amounts** for Carnê-Leão manually or with a separate tool
- **Navigate government portals** that are poorly designed and change
  without notice
- **Store proof of payment** across receipts, PDFs, and screenshots
- **Repeat every month** often from memory

The result is high cognitive load, anxiety around deadlines, and a real risk
of non-compliance for professionals whose core job is clinical practice, not
tax administration.

## What Doesn't Exist

Existing tools fail in one of two ways:

**Too generic.** General personal finance apps (Mobills, Organizze) track
spending but don't understand fiscal obligations. They have no concept of
Carnê-Leão, no Receita Saúde integration, no obligation-specific guidance.

**Too complex.** Accounting software built for businesses (Contabilizei,
QuickBooks) assumes a CNPJ (company registration) or requires a dedicated
accountant. Autonomous PF psychologists don't need an accountant, and don't
even make enough money yet to afford it. They need affordable clarity.

There is no tool designed specifically for the autonomous professional who
knows their obligations exist, but struggles to manage them reliably.

## The Opportunity

A product that:

1. Identifies which obligations apply to each professional's profile
2. Surfaces the right task at the right time, before the deadline
3. Validates completion through document upload, not manual check-off
4. Explains each obligation in plain language, not bureaucratic terms

...would reduce the recurring cognitive load of compliance to close to zero
for this segment.

**Initial focus:** Autonomous psychologists operating as Pessoa Física in
São Paulo, Brazil, a well-defined segment with a shared obligation profile
and no existing dedicated tooling.
