# Launch Strategy

## The Core Challenge

Compasso is a compliance product for a niche professional audience. The challenge isn't awareness, it's trust. A psychologist will not hand over their fiscal life to a product they found through a Facebook ad. They need to hear about it from someone they already trust.

The launch strategy is built around that constraint.

## Target: First 100 Users

Before any paid acquisition, we need 100 users who are genuinely using the product and completing their obligations. These users are the proof that the product works. They are also the primary distribution channel.

**How we get to 100:**

1. **CRP-SP chapter network.** The Conselho Regional de Psicologia de São Paulo runs study groups, ethics committees, and regional events. A single presentation at a CRP event in front of 50 psychologists is worth more than 10,000 Instagram impressions.

2. **Psychology supervision groups.** Clinical supervisors work with groups of 6–12 newer psychologists weekly. A supervisor who adopts Compasso introduces it to every group they run. This is the highest-leverage early channel.

3. **Psychology Instagram & TikTok.** There is a large ecosystem of psychology content creators in Brazil who cover everything from clinical techniques to professional practice. A partnership with 2–3 mid-size accounts (10k–50k followers) focused on autonomous professional life is the right entry point, not generic influencers.

4. **Direct outreach.** The founding team (PM + 1 developer) personally contacts 50 psychologists from their network for the private beta. Personal invitation converts at a rate no ad can match.

## Pricing at Launch

| Plan | Price | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Mensal | R$24,90/mês | Lower commitment, higher churn risk |
| Anual | R$19,90/mês (R$238,80/ano) | 20% discount drives annual commitment |

The annual plan is the target. A user who pays annually has already decided Compasso is worth 12 months of value. Churn from annual subscribers is structurally lower.

**Launch offer:** First 100 users get 3 months free in exchange for weekly feedback sessions. This is not a discount, it's a research investment. The feedback from these 100 users will shape the product more than any amount of analytics.

## Messaging

The product's message at launch is built on one insight from discovery: psychologists are not tax people. They became psychologists to help patients, not to manage fiscal bureaucracy.

**Primary message:**
> "Você é psicólogo, não contador. A Compasso cuida das suas obrigações fiscais para você focar nos seus pacientes."
>
> "You're a psychologist, not and accountant. Compasso takes care of your tax compliance so you can focus on your patients." 

**What we avoid:**
- Technical fiscal language in marketing copy (saves it for inside the product, where it belongs)
- Comparisons to accountants (we're not replacing them for edge cases)
- Generic productivity claims ("save time", "be more organized"), the value is specific: avoid fines, stay compliant

## Content Strategy

A content strategy that supports distribution without requiring a large team:

**Educational content (top of funnel):**
Articles explaining the obligations every autonomous psychologist has, written in plain Portuguese. Target: rank for searches like "carnê-leão psicólogo autônomo" and "INSS psicólogo PF".

These articles serve dual purpose: they bring organic traffic, and they demonstrate product authority. A reader who learns about Carnê-Leão from a Compasso article already associates the brand with fiscal expertise.

**Testimonial content (mid funnel):**
Short-form posts from beta users. Real numbers, real situations. "I've avoided R$165 in fine from february's Carnê-Leão" is more persuasive than any product description.

## What a Successful Launch Looks Like

At the end of the private beta phase (3–6 months after launch):

- 50+ paying users (not trial)
- ≥80% monthly obligation completion rate across the cohort
- ≥3 organic referrals from beta users (unprompted)
- At least one case study documented with user permission
- Clear signal on which acquisition channel has the lowest cost and highest retention

These numbers are not ambitious. They are the minimum signal needed to decide whether to invest in growth or pivot.
